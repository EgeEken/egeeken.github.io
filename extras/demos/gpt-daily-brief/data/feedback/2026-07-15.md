## Puzzle — Two Generals problem

The first proposed approach was a deadline/TTL-style protocol: announce a predetermined attack date, receive an ACK within the deadline, then send an ACK-ACK. The user correctly identified the remaining failure: the receiver of the ACK-ACK must know it arrived before attacking, while the sender cannot know that the ACK-ACK arrived. If the ACK-ACK is lost, the two generals can again make different decisions.

This is the core structure of the impossibility result: every finite acknowledgement chain has a final message whose delivery is known to its receiver but not to its sender. A deadline does not resolve that epistemic asymmetry when messages may be lost permanently; it can only bound waiting or reduce practical risk under additional delivery assumptions.
