### Follow-ups and corrections

- Dollar auction: user correctly observed that players may have individual incentives to continue even when the auction is collectively loss-making. Clarified that $1 is not a rational stopping threshold: if the current losing bid is b and the opponent holds b+$0.05, raising to b+$0.10 wins $1 only if the opponent stops, improving the payoff by $0.90 compared with conceding, independently of b. Whether bidding is rational depends on the opponent's continuation strategy and beliefs; no single absolute cutoff follows from the rules alone.
