### Follow-ups and corrections
- “Verbalized-confidence parse rate” is the fraction of outputs from which the requested numerical confidence can be extracted. In this experiment the prompt required exactly two lines, including `Confidence: <number>`. The 2B 4-bit model often ignored that format and returned only a bare answer, so only 64% of outputs contained a parseable confidence number.
- The Fitch response correctly notes that `q` may be true without anyone knowing it. The standard paradox does not deny that. Its crucial move is applying universal knowability to the larger true proposition `q and nobody knows q`; knowing that conjunction would itself make its second conjunct false. Future puzzle commentary should make that distinction explicit.
