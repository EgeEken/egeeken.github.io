### Follow-ups and corrections
- For Alice's HHT, Bob should choose THH, not HHH. HHH wins only 1/2 of the time, while THH wins 3/4. The asymmetry comes from overlap: whenever HHT is about to occur, its first two Hs also complete THH if they were preceded by T; after any T, Bob needs HH, whereas Alice must build HHT from scratch. The absorbing pattern probabilities are therefore not determined by the two candidate patterns' equal 1/8 frequency on isolated three-toss blocks.
