## Puzzle response — the 1-or-19-minute bus

The user initially reasoned from possible consecutive gap patterns and estimated roughly **4.75 minutes**, noting that short waits might occur in more configurations than the maximum long wait.

The key correction is length-biased sampling. A passenger arriving at a uniformly random time lands in a 1-minute gap with probability **1/20** and in a 19-minute gap with probability **19/20**, because long gaps occupy nineteen times as much clock time. Conditional mean residual waits are 0.5 and 9.5 minutes respectively, so:

`E[W] = (1/20)(0.5) + (19/20)(9.5) = 9.05 minutes.`

Consecutive gap patterns are irrelevant to the residual wait once the containing interval is identified.
