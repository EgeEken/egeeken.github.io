## 2. CS / AI developments

- **Motion forecasting without language or action labels.** *MotionForesight* takes a short monocular observation and predicts future 3D point trajectories on the manipulated object. Its useful trick is supervisory asymmetry: a dense 3D tracker sees the complete video to generate pseudo-ground truth, while the trained forecaster sees only the prefix. Large components remain frozen; learned mask latents and a lightweight adapter carry the forecasting task. The authors report that roughly 40,000 videos outperform systems trained on more than a million clips, including out-of-distribution tests. The main architectural lesson is that a strong offline “teacher” can manufacture geometrically rich targets for a much cheaper causal model. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16192)
- **Long-video models are being taught to recover from looking in the wrong place.** *VideoTreeSearch* represents temporal navigation as a tree and gives the model explicit actions—`zoom_in`, `zoom_out`, `shift` and `answer`. Training includes deliberate wrong-branch detours followed by recovery, rather than only successful demonstrations. Reported gains include **+12.5 mIoU** on CG-Bench, **+7.4 temporal F1** on Haystack-Ego4D and up to **+7.1 accuracy points** on long-video QA. This is more interesting than another token-budget heuristic: backtracking is made part of the action space and training distribution. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16189)

## 3. Technical / research idea — Read-Copy-Update

**Read-Copy-Update (RCU)** is a concurrency pattern for read-mostly data. Instead of modifying a shared object in place, a writer creates or prepares a new version, atomically publishes it, and retires the old version. The crucial split is between **removal** and **reclamation**: readers must stop discovering the old object immediately, but its memory cannot be freed until every reader that could already hold a reference has passed through a grace period.

That asymmetry can make reads extremely cheap—often no lock acquisition, shared write or contended atomic operation—while shifting complexity to writers and lifecycle management. Multiple versions may coexist, publication ordering must be correct, and reclamation errors become use-after-free bugs rather than obvious lock failures.

Outside kernels, the same pattern is useful for hot-swappable routing tables, feature metadata, model-serving configuration and immutable indexes: publish a new snapshot quickly, then reclaim the old one only after in-flight requests are known to be finished. [Linux kernel documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/RCU/whatisRCU.html)

## 4. Non-CS science

- **A molecule’s electronic rearrangement has been resolved before the nuclei can meaningfully respond.** A *Nature Physics* study suddenly ionized para-aminophenol and followed the result with attosecond X-ray absorption at atomic-site resolution. Some non-radiative states decayed in less than a femtosecond; more stable cation states produced a few-femtosecond oscillation interpreted as an electronic wavepacket, before stronger coupling to nuclear motion.
- The importance is methodological. Chemical models often compress electron and nuclear motion into an adiabatic potential-energy surface. These measurements directly expose the earlier, non-adiabatic stage in which correlated electrons reorganize first. That gives theory a sharper benchmark for where the Born–Oppenheimer separation begins to fail. [Nature Physics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-026-03360-x)

## 6. Signal vs noise

**Noise:** treating improved static visual-question answering as evidence that models possess robust visual agency.

**Signal:** *ActiveVision* tests repeated perception, where an agent must decide what to inspect next and detect when its own vision code or intermediate conclusion has failed. Across 17 tasks, the best evaluated configuration solved **10.6%**, scored zero on 11 tasks, while three human participants averaged **96.1%**. The human sample is tiny and the benchmark is authored by its proponents, so the exact headline gap should not be fetishized. The mechanism is still important: code generation does not solve perception when the system cannot notice that the generated detector, crop or measurement is wrong. Closed-loop error recognition is the bottleneck. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16165)

## 7. Marxist fragment

> “society in England daily and hourly commits what the working-men's organs, with perfect correctness, characterise as social murder”

— Friedrich Engels, *The Condition of the Working Class in England* (1845), “Results,” English translation hosted by Marxists Internet Archive. [Primary text](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1845/condition-working-class/ch07.htm)

Engels’s category is stronger than “bad policy”: he is describing preventable mortality produced predictably by durable social arrangements, even when no individual administrator intends a particular death. Applied carefully, it directs attention away from heatwave morality tales about hydration and toward ownership, building standards, care staffing, energy systems and budget allocation. The phrase becomes empty if it substitutes accusation for causal specification; its analytical force lies precisely in tracing those mechanisms.

## 8. Two philosophy / political fragments

### Max Weber — legitimacy is part of the state’s operating system

> “a state is a human community that (successfully) claims the monopoly of the legitimate use of physical force within a given territory.”

— Max Weber, “Politics as a Vocation” (1919), standard English translation. [Primary text](https://www.panarchy.org/weber/politics.html)

“Legitimate” here is sociological, not an endorsement: the state’s claim is treated as valid often enough for commands, taxation and enforcement to reproduce themselves. Capacity therefore depends not only on possessing coercive instruments, but on organizations and beliefs that make their use routine rather than permanently exceptional.

### Rousseau — force becomes durable by becoming obligation

> “The strongest is never strong enough to be always the master, unless he transforms strength into right, and obedience into duty.”

— Jean-Jacques Rousseau, *The Social Contract*, Book I, Chapter III, G. D. H. Cole translation. [Primary text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46333/pg46333-images.html)

Rousseau’s point complements Weber’s: domination economizes on continuous coercion when institutions translate power into a norm people administer themselves. The disagreement begins over whether that transformation can ever create legitimate political obligation, rather than merely stabilize an unequal relation.

## 9. French note — *ce n’est pas faute de…*

**Ce n’est pas faute de + infinitif/noun** means **“not for lack of…”** It asserts that the relevant effort, resource or warning existed, so the explanation for failure lies elsewhere.

- *Ce n’est pas faute d’avoir essayé, mais le modèle diverge dès qu’on augmente la résolution.* — It is not for lack of trying, but the model diverges as soon as we increase the resolution.
- Spoken French commonly drops *ne*: *C’est pas faute de t’avoir prévenu.*

Contrast: *faute de temps* means “for lack of time”; *ce n’est pas faute de temps* rejects lack of time as the explanation. [Académie française dictionary via CNRTL](https://www.cnrtl.fr/definition/academie9/faute)

## 11. Unrelated fresh fact — fertilizer scarcity once became a territorial doctrine

In the nineteenth century, accumulated seabird **guano** was valuable enough to be treated as strategic mineral wealth. In an era before industrial nitrogen fixation, it supplied concentrated nitrogen and phosphate to exhausted agricultural soils.

The **U.S. Guano Islands Act of 1856** authorized the president, at discretion, to treat an unoccupied guano-bearing island discovered by a U.S. citizen—provided it was not under another government’s jurisdiction—as “appertaining” to the United States. Agronomic nutrient scarcity was thus converted directly into a mechanism for overseas territorial claims. The law is a compact example of how a mundane material bottleneck can generate legal categories and imperial reach. [U.S. Code](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title48-section1411) · [Smithsonian](https://americanhistory.si.edu/press/releases/national-museum-american-history-opens-exhibit-norie-marine-atlas-and-guano-trade)

## 12. Puzzle — Parrondo’s paradox

You have capital C, initially an integer.

- **Game A:** win €1 with probability **0.497**; otherwise lose €1.
- **Game B:** if C is divisible by 3, win €1 with probability **0.097**; otherwise win with probability **0.747**. Lose €1 in the remaining cases.

Played indefinitely by itself, **A loses**. Under its own long-run distribution of capital modulo 3, **B also loses**.

Yet a random mixture—choose A or B with probability 1/2 each turn—can have **positive** expected long-run drift.

**How can two losing games combine into a winning one?** Which hidden state is being redistributed when you switch games, and why is “each game has negative expectation” not sufficient unless the expectation is conditioned on the same state distribution? [Background](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03089)
