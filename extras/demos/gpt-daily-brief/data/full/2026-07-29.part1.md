## 2. CS and AI developments

### A decoder-only model in which depth, edges and captions are all first-class outputs

Most “unified” multimodal systems still contain an asymmetry: images enter through one route, text is decoded through another, and dense outputs such as depth or segmentation require task-specific heads. **MODUS** instead treats a diverse set of modalities symmetrically inside one decoder-only model, without modality-specific heads, losses or separate task pipelines. The reported system can accept and emit combinations of RGB, depth, surface normals, segmentation, detections, edges, captions and learned visual features.

The architectural payoff is not merely fewer heads. A model can chain through an intermediate modality—image → depth → reconstruction, for example—or generate two views of the same scene and check their cross-modal consistency. That turns generation into a possible verification instrument rather than only an output format. The unresolved trade-off is whether a common token-and-loss interface can preserve the calibration and spatial precision that specialised predictors get from purpose-built objectives. [Apple research page](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/modus) · [Paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=t7kTpjsRIw)

### Learning closed-loop driving behaviour in a deliberately crude visual world

**Gigapixel** attacks a different bottleneck in end-to-end driving: offline human trajectories do not expose a policy to enough of its own mistakes. Its simulator therefore renders simplified perspective-view scenes—essentially a structured bounding-box world—at about **50,000 agent steps per second**, rather than spending the budget on photorealism. A privileged reinforcement-learning teacher explores in self-play; a pixel policy is trained through on-policy DAgger-style distillation, then adapted to real sensor appearance with a lighter perception stage.

The design separates two problems that are usually entangled: learning interactive closed-loop behaviour and matching real-image statistics. The paper reports competitive HUGSIM and NAVSIM-v2 performance without human trajectory supervision, but this is not evidence that crude simulation contains every safety-relevant causal variable. Its stronger claim is methodological: if the simulation preserves the geometry and interaction structure needed for control, appearance transfer can be postponed instead of making photorealistic rendering the price of every policy update. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19641)

## 3. Technical idea: Dancing Links and reversible search state

An **exact-cover** problem asks you to select rows of a binary matrix so that every column contains exactly one selected `1`. Sudoku, polyomino tiling and many scheduling constraints can be encoded this way. **Algorithm X** solves the problem by choosing a column, trying one row that covers it, deleting every conflicting row and covered column, then backtracking.

The expensive part of naive backtracking is repeatedly copying or rebuilding the reduced matrix. Donald Knuth’s **Dancing Links (DLX)** representation stores only the `1` entries in circular doubly linked lists, connected horizontally by row and vertically by column. “Covering” a constraint splices its nodes out of neighbouring lists. “Uncovering” performs the inverse pointer assignments in reverse order, restoring the exact previous state without a snapshot.

This is a useful general search pattern: make speculative mutations **locally reversible**, then use the data structure itself as the undo log. DLX also makes the “choose the column with the fewest remaining rows” heuristic cheap, often reducing the search tree far more than a sophisticated scoring function would. The technique is pointer-heavy and not automatically cache-friendly, but for sparse exact-cover instances its combination of representation, heuristic and rollback is unusually clean. [Knuth, “Dancing Links”](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0011047)

## 4. Non-CS science: ordinary processed films can make linear polarization direction-dependent

Optical non-reciprocity means that light does not experience the same response when its propagation direction is reversed. It usually requires magneto-optic fields, elaborate metamaterials or other specialised structures. A new *Nature Materials* paper shows a route in comparatively simple solution-processed films made from CdS, CdSe and CdTe magic-size clusters.

The mechanism is **interference between chirality and linear anisotropy**. If a material has comparable circular and linear dichroism, reversing the optical path can change the absorption—and even emission—of orthogonal linearly polarised states. The authors demonstrate this experimentally and derive symmetry-based design rules for when the effect appears.

That does not yet amount to a drop-in optical isolator: magnitude, losses, wavelength range, fabrication tolerance and device integration remain engineering questions. But it changes the design space. Direction-dependent routing or polarisation-multiplexed encoding may not require a conventionally “exotic” non-reciprocal medium if two ordinary anisotropies are deliberately made to interfere. [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41563-026-02660-0)

## 5. Geopolitics and political economy

### Municipal administration is becoming a recurring legal battlefield in Turkey

Turkish authorities detained Üsküdar mayor Sinem Dedetaş and five others this morning over alleged irregularities in building and occupancy permits. Police reportedly searched eleven addresses and seized digital material; the allegation is that contractors were pushed into consultancy agreements to obtain approvals. The opposition calls the broader wave of municipal cases politically motivated, while the government maintains that the judiciary is independent.

The institutional point is wider than another arrest count. Opposition municipalities possess budgets, procurement networks, planning authority and visible service-delivery capacity, but those same functions generate dense administrative records that can be investigated selectively and repeatedly. National incumbency therefore confers an asymmetric advantage: central prosecutors and police can convert the ordinary documentary surface of local government—permits, tenders, contractors, payrolls—into sustained political risk.

This does not establish whether the specific allegations are true or false. It explains why municipal victories do not automatically translate into secure rival centres of power. Local office gives the opposition an organisational base, but also exposes that base to legal supervision exercised through institutions it does not control. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/turkey-detains-another-opposition-district-mayor-others-crackdown-expands-2026-07-29/)

### Turkey is trying to move from pipeline corridor to upstream participant in Iraqi oil

The old Iraq–Turkey pipeline agreement expired on Monday without the expected extension being signed. At the same Ankara meeting, however, Turkey’s state producer TPAO acquired a **15% stake** in BP Energy Company of Kirkuk Limited and will participate in operating the Kirkuk field. Erdoğan says a broader energy agreement should follow.

That is a more consequential shift than a transit-fee negotiation. A pipeline state earns from geography; an upstream equity partner gains access to production decisions, field information and a share of project returns. For Ankara, the strategic objective is therefore not merely to reopen or fill Kirkuk–Ceyhan, but to couple the export route with a Turkish position inside the producing asset.

The bargain is still unfinished. Pipeline rules, arbitration history, Baghdad–Erbil revenue disputes, investment requirements and physical output remain separate constraints. But the TPAO stake gives Turkey a durable institutional foothold even while the interstate transport agreement is unresolved. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/erdogan-says-turkey-aims-sign-energy-cooperation-deal-with-iraq-2026-07-28/)

## 6. Signal versus noise: reserve potential is not deliverable supply

The **signal** is TPAO’s 15% operating stake: ownership and field participation are concrete assets.

The **noise** is reading “approximately 3 billion barrels of reserve potential” or political discussion of very high daily flows as oil already available to Turkey. A resource estimate is not a production profile, and a pipeline’s nameplate capacity is not its realised throughput. Field development, decline rates, contracts, security, Baghdad–Erbil allocation, the expired transit agreement and outstanding arbitration all sit between geological potential and a barrel delivered at Ceyhan. The useful metrics are sanctioned investment, booked recoverable reserves, actual field output, contracted export volumes and sustained pipeline utilisation. ## 7. Marxist fragment

> “All revolutions perfected this machine instead of breaking it.”

— Karl Marx, *The Eighteenth Brumaire of Louis Bonaparte*, chapter VII. [Primary text](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1852/18th-brumaire/ch07.htm)

Marx is describing the centralised bureaucratic, military and executive apparatus accumulated by the French monarchy, revolution, Napoleon and the regimes that followed. Each successive political victor captured and enlarged machinery originally built to dissolve local powers and administer society from the centre.
