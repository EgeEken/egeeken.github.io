## 2. CS / AI developments

- **Some VLMs appear to bind geometry to language through linear “spatial IDs.”** Kang and colleagues find vectors that encode object location while attached to textual activations; interventions on those vectors can systematically alter a model’s spatial beliefs. They report an analogous temporal-ID mechanism in video models. The important implication is architectural: visual geometry may be converted into an address-like representation and then manipulated mainly inside the language stream, rather than remaining a distinct geometric workspace. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12626) · [ICLR page](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/poster/10011690)
- **Video encoders can preserve time while the projector destroys it.** An Arrow-of-Time diagnostic separates the visual encoder, multimodal projector and LLM. Video-centric encoders retained strong direction-of-time information, but performance often collapsed after projection; a Q-Former was especially lossy, while a time-preserving MLP made the signal usable downstream. This is a useful systems result because it changes where to spend capacity: improving the backbone is pointless when the interface discards the feature. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07568)

## 3. Technical / research idea — algebraic effects and handlers

An **algebraic effect** lets code announce an operation—such as `yield`, `choose`, `read`, `raise` or `await`—without fixing how that operation is implemented. The enclosing **handler** supplies the interpretation and may receive a delimited continuation representing “the rest of the computation.” Unlike a normal exception handler, it can resume that continuation, replace its result, run it several times or decline to resume it.

This separates *what a computation requests* from *what the runtime or library does about it*. The same nondeterministic `choose` operation can be handled by taking the first branch, enumerating all branches, sampling randomly or constructing a search tree. Languages such as Koka use typed handlers to express generators and async control as composable libraries rather than unrelated compiler features. The main engineering hazard is continuation ownership: multi-shot resumption can duplicate side effects or resource use, so practical systems often distinguish one-shot from reusable continuations. [Plotkin & Pretnar](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.1399) · [Koka documentation](https://koka-lang.github.io/koka/doc/book.html#sec-effect-handlers)

## 4. Non-CS science

- **A display-manufacturing method deliberately creates cracks to obtain cleaner quantum-dot pixels.** Conventional transfer printing has difficulty separating dense quantum-dot films without deformation or colour contamination. The new process uses a patterned stamp to nucleate nanoscale cracks that break cohesive bonds between neighbouring dots at controlled boundaries.
- The group reports pixel sizes down to **600 nm**, uniform patterning over areas up to **4 inches**, integration of cadmium-free RGB pixels onto non-flat active-matrix backplanes, and a flexible device. ## 7. Marxist fragment

> “A democratic republic is the best possible political shell for capitalism”

— V. I. Lenin, *The State and Revolution*, Chapter 1 (1917), Progress Publishers translation. [Primary text](https://www.marxists.org/archive/lenin/works/1917/staterev/ch01.htm)

Lenin’s claim is not that constitutional form is irrelevant or that every regime is identical. It is that capitalist power can become more stable when it no longer depends on a monarch or a visibly personal command: finance, appointments, lobbying, ownership and administrative continuity operate through institutions that survive electoral turnover. The argument should therefore be tested structurally—by asking which decisions remain insulated from changes of office—rather than used as a slogan that erases meaningful democratic differences.

## 8. Two philosophy / political fragments

### Mary Wollstonecraft — remedy the structure, not its casualties

> “It is justice, not charity, that is wanting in the world!”

— Mary Wollstonecraft, *A Vindication of the Rights of Woman*, Chapter 4 (1792). [Primary text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/3420/pg3420.html)

Wollstonecraft is discussing women pushed into dependence and then morally condemned for the consequences. Charity treats deprivation as an exceptional misfortune and preserves the giver’s discretion; justice asks which educational, property and social arrangements systematically produced dependence in the first place.

### W. E. B. Du Bois — the colour line is institutional

> “the problem of the Twentieth Century is the problem of the color line”

— W. E. B. Du Bois, *The Souls of Black Folk*, “The Forethought” (1903). [Primary text](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/408/408-h/408-h.htm)

The “colour line” is not reducible to interpersonal prejudice. Across the book it organizes citizenship, labour, schooling, land, political representation and access to the social surplus. That makes the concept portable: a hierarchy persists when many formally separate institutions reproduce the same boundary.

## 9. French note — *j’avoue*

In recent informal French, standalone **j’avoue** often works as an agreement marker rather than a literal confession: **“true,” “fair,” “I know, right?”**

- *Le dernier épisode était beaucoup mieux. — J’avoue.*  
  “The last episode was much better. — True.”
- *Ah ouais, j’avoue, ça se tient.*  
  “Oh yeah, fair, that makes sense.”

Keep it for casual speech and messages. In formal or professional contexts, use *c’est vrai*, *je suis d’accord* or *je reconnais que…* depending on the meaning. [Usage note](https://www.connexionfrance.com/magazine/learning-french-what-does-javoue-mean-and-when-should-it-be-used/694150)

## 11. Unrelated fresh fact — the first recorded strike was paid in grain

Around **1157 BCE**, the specialist tomb-builders of Deir el-Medina stopped work after their grain rations—effectively their wages—were repeatedly delayed. They left the royal necropolis and staged sit-ins near temples and administrative buildings. The action is preserved in the Turin Strike Papyrus, probably written by the scribe Amennakht.

These were not marginal day labourers but skilled state workers responsible for royal tombs. Their leverage came from concentrated expertise and the ideological importance of the work: a state that claimed to maintain *ma’at*, social and cosmic order, was failing its own obligations. The record matters because it shows collective withdrawal of labour appearing not as a modern industrial invention, but as a response to a breakdown in an ancient redistributive payroll system. [Background and papyrus account](https://www.worldhistory.org/article/1089/the-first-labor-strike-in-history/)

## 12. Puzzle — a Condorcet cycle

Three voters rank three policies:

- Voter 1: **A > B > C**
- Voter 2: **B > C > A**
- Voter 3: **C > A > B**

Pairwise majority votes produce:

- **A beats B**, 2–1.
- **B beats C**, 2–1.
- **C beats A**, 2–1.

There is no majority-best option; collective preference cycles even though every individual ranking is internally consistent.
