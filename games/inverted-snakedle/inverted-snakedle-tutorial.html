Inverted Snakedle Tutorial

Your goal is to collect as many fruits as possible before you run out of moves
Press these buttons to move, undo and clear
Alternatively, on the keyboard:
- WASD or Arrow keys for movement
- Z or Backspace for Undo
- C or Escape for clear
Inverted rule
1- Eating fruit makes you grow
Inverted rule
2- Hitting a wall or your own tail makes you crash
Scoring and Share
Once you've completed a puzzle, you can submit your solution and share it with others (or me! make sure to send your results and feedback to me!)

In Inverted Snakedle there is no auto-submit. You can submit any run to score how many fruits you collected before the move limit.
