Fruitle Tutorial

You are the fruits now. Survive as many turns as possible while the snake hunts you.
Turn order
1- Click a fruit to select it
2- Choose a direction with arrows / WASD
3- Press ✓ to send that fruit move
4- Snake then takes multiple steps on its optimal route
5- Repeat until all fruits are eaten
Controls
- Click fruit on map to select it
- WASD or Arrow keys picks direction
- Enter sends turn (same as ✓ button)
- Z or Backspace undoes the previous full turn
- In the main game, C or Escape clears the whole move queue and resets the map (no C/Escape action is implemented in this tutorial)
Goal
Survive turns, not optimal moves. Share text reports survived turns; for infinite-run wins it also includes remaining fruits.
Scoring and Share
Performance bands are by survived turns (< 5 red, 5-9 orange, 10-14 yellow, 15-19 green, 20+ blue). Purple is reserved for infinite-run win cases.
